# Zunara — Free Astrology Tools & Daily Horoscopes

**[zunara.vercel.app](https://zunara.vercel.app)** — free, accurate astrology tools and horoscopes calculated from real planetary positions rather than generic copy.

## Live at
- **Website:** https://zunara.vercel.app
- **Free Astrology Tools hub:** https://zunara.vercel.app/tools

## What's included (100% free, no sign-up)
- **Birth Chart Calculator** — exact natal chart (Sun, Moon, rising signs, houses, aspects) from real planetary positions and birthplace longitude.
- **Compatibility (Synastry)** — two real birth charts scored across four relationship dimensions from the actual planetary angles between them.
- **Your Sky** — a personal dashboard from your saved chart: today's strongest influences and the next 30 days in plain English.
- **Sky Now** — where every planet is at this moment: positions, signs, retrogrades, lunar phase, major aspects.
- **Interactive Sky Map** — the Sun, Moon, planets and brightest stars above your own coordinates, plotted from precise astronomy.
- **Daily Ephemeris** — day-by-day planetary positions for the Sun, Moon, eight planets and lunar nodes.
- **Sky Events Calendar** — the year's real eclipses, meteor showers, full moons and planetary alignments, verified against astronomical calculation.
- **Retrograde Tracker** — which planets are backtracking right now and what it means.
- **Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Yearly Horoscopes** for all 12 signs.
- **Famous Birthdays by Date** — who shares your birthday, verified from Wikidata.
- **Astrology Glossary & Guide** — planets, signs, houses, aspects and movement terms in plain English.

## Tech
- **Framework:** Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript
- **Astronomy engine:** `astronomy-engine` — no invented coordinates, real planetary theory
- **Data:** deterministic calculations + Wikidata-verified celebrity data
- **i18n:** 6 locales (English, Urdu, Arabic, Spanish, Chinese, Hindi, RTL support)
- **Deployed on:** Vercel

## Why we exist
Every position you read on Zunara is computed from astronomical theory — never invented. We're equally clear about the line between what the math measures (planetary positions) and the meaning we reflect on (astrology, written honestly for reflection and entertainment).
